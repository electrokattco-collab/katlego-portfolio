Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?
Most one-page websites are ready within 3–5 days after I receive all your information. If you need changes, each round typically takes 1–2 days.
What do I need to provide?
Just your business details: name, what you do, contact info, and any photos you have. If you don't have photos, I can help you find stock images or take screenshots of your social media.
Do I need to pay upfront?
For websites under R1000, full payment upfront. For larger projects, 50% upfront and 50% on completion. I accept EFT, Cash Send, or cash if you're local to Meyerspark/Pretoria.
Can I update the website myself later?
I build simple HTML/CSS sites that are easy to edit. I'll show you how to change text and photos. For major changes, you can always WhatsApp me.
What if I don't like the design?
I send you a preview before the site goes live. You get 2 rounds of changes included in the price. We'll work together until you're happy with it.
Do you do complex websites with booking systems?
Currently I focus on simple one-page sites and basic booking forms. For complex features like payment gateways or advanced booking systems, I can refer you to other developers.
